# Solarity AI: company one-pager

<img src="../../brand/logos/solarity-sun-mark-on-dark-192.png" alt="Solarity AI sun mark" width="72">

**Building for human potential.** Solarity AI builds AI self-improvement apps and the tools that let its small team ship them fast.

## What we do

- **4 consumer apps** for self-care, cognitive practice, sleep, and fitness, all live on iOS and Android.
- **3 SaaS products** for the engineering, management, and marketing work behind mobile apps, built alongside our own apps.

## Track record

**4 apps in 12 weeks.** Looks Max launched on June 23, 2026, Fitness Max on August 15, IQ Max on September 11, and Sleep Max on September 16. Dates are App Store release dates. Every app is also on Google Play.

## How we work

- **Give every product a clear purpose.** Start with a defined audience and a useful place in everyday life.
- **Make the next useful action clear.** Design is part of how the product works.
- **Reuse the work. Keep the judgment.** Build workflows that can serve more than one product, while keeping the decisions each audience needs.

## Business model

- **Apps:** subscriptions and in-app purchases. Current plans and prices are on each store listing.
- **SaaS:** Management Max has a free plan for 1 app and paid plans for larger portfolios. Engineering Max is free during early access. Marketing Max has no price yet.
