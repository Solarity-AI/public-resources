# Privacy policy

**The current policy is at [solarityai.com/privacy](https://solarityai.com/privacy/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=public-resources&utm_campaign=legal-public-privacy-policy)** (effective October 26, 2026). It is the only authoritative version.

## Summary

- **Scope:** the solarityai.com website and the ways you contact Solarity AI LLC from it.
- **What we receive:** job applications, emails, calendar bookings, and Google Form responses. When you visit, Cloudflare, which hosts the site, handles the IP address and browser type every web request carries, to deliver the site and protect it from abuse.
- **No cookies:** the website sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. Cloudflare Web Analytics counts page views without cookies.
- **Use:** we use what you send only for the reason you sent it. We don't sell or rent personal information, and we don't use it for advertising.
- **Who else handles it:** FormSubmit forwards careers applications to our inbox, after a Google reCAPTCHA check that sees your IP address and browser details. Google runs the booking page and forms, Cloudflare hosts the website, and our email provider stores the messages you send. We share personal information with anyone else only when the law requires it.
- **Apps and SaaS:** each app has its own privacy policy, linked from its App Store and Google Play listing. Each SaaS product has its own policy on its site.

To see, correct, or delete your information, email [ata.turhan@solarityai.com](mailto:ata.turhan@solarityai.com).

*This summary is for convenience only. If it differs from the policy on solarityai.com, the policy on the website applies.*
