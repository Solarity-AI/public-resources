# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participating in Solarity AI's community, in this repository and anywhere we represent the company, to be welcoming and free of harassment for everyone. That includes people of every age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, appearance, race, religion, and sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the people we build for.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insults, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This code applies in this repository's issues, pull requests, and discussions. It also applies when someone officially represents Solarity AI in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to [ata.turhan@solarityai.com](mailto:ata.turhan@solarityai.com). All reports are reviewed promptly and handled confidentially.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits, or other contributions that break this code. They may also warn, temporarily ban, or permanently ban anyone involved, depending on how serious the behavior is.
