# Brand guidelines

These rules come from the [solarityai.com press kit](https://solarityai.com/press/).

## The name

- Write **Solarity AI**: two words, with "AI" in capitals.
- The legal name is **Solarity AI LLC**. Use it in legal and formal contexts.
- Not: *SolarityAI*, *Solarity Ai*, *SOLARITY*, *Solarity.ai*.

## The sun mark

- Use it as supplied, from [`../logos/`](../logos/).
- Don't recolor, stretch, rotate, outline, add effects to, or redraw it.
- Don't put it on busy images or light backgrounds where the yellow loses contrast.

## Describing Solarity AI

- Use the approved descriptions in [`../../press-kit/`](../../press-kit/).
- Describe products only with the facts on [solarityai.com](https://solarityai.com/).
- **Ask before stating numbers, users, or results** that aren't on the website. Email [ata.turhan@solarityai.com](mailto:ata.turhan@solarityai.com).

## Visual style

- A dark, warm background (`#0F0D0B`), cream text, and gold accents. See [`../colors/`](../colors/).
- Newsreader for headlines and Manrope for text. See [`../typography/`](../typography/).
- Calm and direct. Short headlines, plain sentences, no hype.
